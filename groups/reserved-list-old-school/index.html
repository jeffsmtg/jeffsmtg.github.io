Reserved List cards that are Old School 93/94 legal

207 unique cards are on the Reserved List and also legal in Old School 93/94, using Eternal Central (North American) set membership. Old School 93/94 legal means the card has a paper printing in the Eternal Central (North American) set list: Alpha, Beta, Unlimited, Revised (including Summer Magic and Foreign Black Border), Arabian Nights, Antiquities, Legends, The Dark, and Fallen Empires. This is not the Swedish list, which omits Fallen Empires. Banned and restricted tags follow Scryfall's Old School listings; Power Nine cards that Scryfall left untagged are marked restricted, matching the rest of the Power Nine. Every printing of these cards is listed in the explorer.
